Course 01

Hindustani Vocal

Our Hindustani Vocal classes introduce students to the fundamentals and progressive aspects of Hindustani music. Students are guided through practical singing as well as the theoretical aspects of music.

Training begins with swar and alankar and the basic exercises that build a reliable voice, then moves on to raga, tala and classical compositions as the student's command of the material grows.

Course 02

Tabla Instrument Classes

Tabla is one of the important percussion instruments in Indian classical music. Our Tabla classes provide students with systematic training in rhythm, technique, compositions and performance.

Students work through the basic strokes and bols before taking on taal, laya and composition — and learn to accompany vocal and instrumental music, which is where a tabla player's understanding of rhythm is truly tested.

Course 03

Bharatanatyam Classes

Bharatanatyam is one of India's major classical dance traditions, combining rhythm, movement, expression, music and storytelling.

At Shree Guru Sangeet Vidyalaya, students are introduced to the fundamentals of Bharatanatyam through systematic training and regular practice — beginning with adavus, mudras and posture, and progressing to abhinaya, traditional compositions and performance on stage.

Examination & Certification

Preparation and guidance for examinations

For students pursuing certification, the institute provides guidance and preparation for applicable examinations. The institute has been providing examination-oriented training associated with Bharati Vidyapeeth, Pune, Maharashtra, subject to the applicable course and examination requirements.

Examination preparation includes
