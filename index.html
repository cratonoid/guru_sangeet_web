About Us

An institute devoted to India's classical traditions

Shree Guru Sangeet Vidyalaya, Ranebennur, is an institute dedicated to teaching and promoting Indian classical music and Bharatanatyam.

Our aim is to provide students with a strong foundation in traditional Indian performing arts through regular practice, systematic teaching, and dedicated guidance.

We welcome students who wish to learn for personal interest as well as those who want to pursue structured learning and certification. Whether you are learning for cultural enrichment, performance, or a formal qualification, our programmes are designed to give you structured and guided learning.

Online & Offline

Learn from wherever you are

We offer both online and offline classes to provide flexibility for students. Whichever you choose, the focus remains on systematic learning, regular practice and individual guidance.

✉

Online Classes

Students can learn from the comfort of their homes through live online sessions. Online classes suit students who:

  • Live outside Ranebennur
  • Have scheduling or travel limitations
  • Prefer learning remotely
  • Want to continue regular practice from home
☖

Offline / In-Person Classes

Students in and around Ranebennur can attend in-person classes and learn directly with the teacher. In-person learning offers:

  • Direct, hands-on correction from the teacher
  • Group practice alongside fellow students
  • Access to instruments at the centre
  • Participation in institute events and performances

Examination & Certification

Guidance through the examination process

For students pursuing certification, the institute provides guidance and preparation for applicable examinations. The institute has been providing examination-oriented training associated with Bharati Vidyapeeth, Pune, Maharashtra, subject to the applicable course and examination requirements.

Examination preparation includes

  • Practical preparation
  • Theory preparation
  • Revision
  • Syllabus-oriented training
  • Examination guidance
  • Assistance with the process
For Certification Students

Online Examination Portal

As part of the updated examination process, students may need to use an official online web portal for examination-related activities. Students pursuing certification courses can access the designated portal for applicable examination processes.

Please follow the instructions provided on the official portal regarding registration, application, examination dates, fees, documents, eligibility and other requirements.
